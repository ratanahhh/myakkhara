MyAkkhara — Draw & Label

Phase 1: draw boxes around every line first, then switch to Label to type them all.

Get started: click "Load Page(s)" and select one or more full page photos.

Draw a box around every line across all your pages first (fast pass, no typing). Click a box to select it: drag its edges/corners to resize, drag its middle to move it, or press Delete to remove it. Then switch to the "Label" tab to type each one, shown big and one at a time.
